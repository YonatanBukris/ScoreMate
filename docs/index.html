1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the ScoreMate mobile application (“ScoreMate”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the App as distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

In short: ScoreMate does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not ask you to create an account, we do not request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location, and we do not operate any server that receives your data.

1.1 Information we do not collect

ScoreMate does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses;
  • Account credentials — the App has no login or registration;
  • Contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or precise or coarse location data;
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID), and we do not display third-party advertising;
  • Analytics, behavioural tracking, or cross-app / cross-site tracking data.

1.2 Data stored on your device

All content you create in ScoreMate — including game scores, player or team names you type in, match and round history, and your custom settings such as theme, language, sound and haptics preferences — is stored locally on your device using the operating system’s local storage for apps. This data never leaves your device through ScoreMate and is not accessible to us.

Because this data is local, it is under your control: you can erase it at any time by clearing the App’s data from within the App (where offered) or by uninstalling ScoreMate. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes locally stored data. Locally stored data may also be included in your device’s own backups (for example iCloud Backup or Google Backup) if you have enabled them; those backups are governed by Apple’s and Google’s policies, not by us.

1.4 Children’s privacy

ScoreMate is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because no personal information is collected, there is no such information for a parent or guardian to review or request deletion of.

Privacy laws such as the EU / UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) give you rights over personal data held about you, including rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability, and a right not to have your personal information sold or shared. If you wish to exercise rights regarding purchase records, please contact Apple, Google, or RevenueCat, who act as the processors of that data.

1.6 Security

Because your ScoreMate data stays on your device, its security depends primarily on your device’s own protections. We recommend keeping your device passcode or biometric lock enabled and your operating system up to date.
